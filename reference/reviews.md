  not something documented for anyone to grab. Since CCS is already a listed
  provider there, this needs asking homecare.co.uk directly or checking
  whatever provider account access CCS already has — not something I can
  confirm exists without account access.

## Rules for every use
- Verbatim only. Light trims marked with "..." — never reword, never merge reviewers.
- Attribution: initials or first name + relationship + source + date.
- Staff reviews never appear on family-facing pages.
- Anything naming a carer or identifying a person we support needs a consent check before it goes on the website, even if already published elsewhere.
- Never mark third-party reviews up in JSON-LD (schema gets the CQC facts, not these).
- Our own copy still avoids banned words even when a quote contains them.
