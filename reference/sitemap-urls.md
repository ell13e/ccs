## Redirect rules at launch

1. **One 301 per old URL** — never chain. Use the `At launch` column on go-live day; the inventory table is the complete list.
2. **Never 301 to a URL that does not exist yet** — if `New URL` is P2 or P3, point `At launch` at the nearest live P1 ancestor (e.g. `/about-us/` for about children, `/care-services/` for service children) or `/` when no P1 parent exists (advice cluster, utility pages).
3. **Update redirects when pages ship** — when a P2/P3 page goes live, change its old URLs from the `At launch` target to the `New URL` column value.
4. **Services split** — `/home/home-care-services-kent/` → `/care-services/` only. Eight new service child URLs are net-new; link them internally, do not redirect the old URL to each one.
5. **Drop the `/home/` prefix** on all utility and content pages in new URLs.
6. **Under construction** — 410 Gone or 301 to `/`. Do not recreate.
