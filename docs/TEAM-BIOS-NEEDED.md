# Team profile pages: words needed

Each person on Who you'll meet now has a profile page, for example
`/who-youll-meet/victoria-walker/`. Each page shows their photo, role, a line
about them, a "Book a free consultation" button and the rest of the team.
Victoria's page also has the company story video.

Each page has an "About [first name]" section that comes from the page's own
editor (Pages > Who You'll Meet > [name]). It is empty because nothing has been
written for anyone. Until a page has some words:

- the "About" section is hidden;
- the page is kept out of Google and out of the sitemap, so a page with only a
  name and a photo is not indexed as a thin page.

When something is typed into the editor and the page is updated, both change
automatically.
