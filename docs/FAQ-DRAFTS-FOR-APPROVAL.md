## Domiciliary care

**What help can I have during a visit?**
Personal care (washing, dressing and grooming), medication reminders and
administration, preparing meals and help with eating, light housekeeping and
laundry, shopping and errands, and company and conversation.

**What does domiciliary care cost?**
From £36.38 an hour on weekdays between 7am and 10pm, and £43.31 an hour at
weekends. Overnight hours (10pm to 7am) are £42.16 on weekdays and £51.54 at
weekends. There is a one-off £30 fee when we set up your care, and bank
holidays are charged at double.

**Can my visits fit around my day?**
Yes. A morning visit to help you get ready, an evening visit to help you wind
down, or both. We build the care plan around your routine.

**Will the same carers come each time?**
You see the same small team, who get to know how you like things done.

**Is there a minimum visit length?** **Please confirm:** your homecare.co.uk
listing says 60 minutes.

## Respite care

**How long can respite care last?**
From a few hours to a day, overnight or for several days, as regular breaks
(weekly or fortnightly), or as holiday cover for a regular carer.

**Can you help at short notice?**
Emergency respite care is one of the options we offer. **Please confirm** how
quickly you can usually start in an emergency, so the answer can say so.

**Will their routine carry on while I'm away?**
We learn the routines, preferences and needs of the person you care for before
you go, so the handover is smooth and their day carries on as normal.

**What does respite care cost?**
Daytime care is £36.38 an hour on weekdays and £43.31 at weekends. If a carer
stays the night, a sleep-in night is £242.49 and a waking night is £323.00,
each at a fixed rate.

## Complex care

**Which conditions do you support?**
Epilepsy and seizure management, spinal injuries and paralysis, tracheostomy
care, PEG feeding and nutrition support, ventilation support, cerebral palsy,
brain injuries including acquired brain injury, multiple sclerosis and motor
neurone disease.

**Can you provide care through the night?**
Yes: night sits and waking nights. A complex sleep-in night is £287.74 on
weekdays and £307.82 at weekends; a complex waking night is £323.00 on weekdays
and £350.92 at weekends.

**Do you work with the person's other professionals?**
Yes. We work alongside occupational therapists, physiotherapists, speech and
language therapists, community nurses, GPs, consultants, social workers and
the family.

**What does complex care cost by the hour?**
From £40.43 an hour on weekdays between 7am and 10pm and £48.51 at weekends.
Overnight hours are £46.20 on weekdays and £53.13 at weekends.
