# Doodles

Hand-drawn pictures for `ccs_doodle()` (inc/icons.php). Each is one drawing from a
vector pack the client supplied on 20 November 2026 (zip downloads dated 22 October
2026), recoloured to the brand plum (#564298), with speckled shading and small
hatching marks removed and its lines made heavier and rounder to match the
arrows, and saved as a transparent WebP no larger than 360px. The packs themselves are not in the theme.

The client holds the licence for these packs. If they came from a subscription
library, the download may need registering against this project in that account.
